# v0.7.0 — Local transfers (release candidate)

The approved business dashboard now includes a synthetic local transfer flow backed by SwiftTransferKit. It validates whole-rupiah amounts and recipient details, presents an immutable review, and supports status lookup after a lost submission response.

## Manual acceptance walkthrough

1. Approve the synthetic business and open **Transfer lokal**.
2. Enter a demo recipient, account and positive integer amount. Enable **Simulasikan respons hilang**.
3. Review the recipient and amount, then confirm once.
4. Verify **Hasil belum diketahui** appears and the new-transfer action is unavailable.
5. Return to the dashboard, reopen the transfer and check the original recipient.
6. Tap **Periksa status transfer** and verify **Diterima untuk diproses**.

## Scope and trade-offs

Acceptance is distinct from settlement. The simulator does not route funds or change dashboard balances. The SDK journals the transfer intent and key; the app retains the reviewed recipient and simulated bank for the current process. Relaunch recovery, real bank integration, fee schedules and bank-specific account rules are outside this milestone. The simulator workflow requires no real account details.

The release remains a prototype demonstration. Publishing a release is a separate step after verification.
